# 🐧 Free Linux Course: earn your terminal black belt

Welcome to the **dojo**.

Here, you don't read 40 pages of theory before touching a terminal. You learn Linux by typing commands, breaking things and solving challenges. A CTF is waiting for you as early as chapter 0.

You walk in a **white belt**. You walk out able to run a Linux server.

**[▶ Enter the dojo](00-welcome.html)** · [🇫🇷 Passer en français](../fr/)

100% free · no signup · nothing to install · open source

---

## 🥋 Dojo rules

1. **No AI during exercises.** You're here to learn, not to copy.
2. **Practice in a sandbox.** A VM, or a free Ubuntu VM right in your browser ([Killercoda](https://killercoda.com/playgrounds/scenario/ubuntu), [Google Cloud Shell](https://shell.cloud.google.com)).
3. **Stuck? Search.** Read the `man` page, test, try again. That's where you grow.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. No signup, no paywall. The course is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/N0vachr0n0/Hands-on-Linux-course).

**Do I need to install Linux?**
No. A free Ubuntu VM runs in your browser, on Windows, macOS or even a tablet.

**Any prerequisites?**
Know how to use a computer and want to type commands. The dojo handles the rest.

**Found a mistake, have an idea?**
Every chapter has a feedback form, and contributions are welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/N0vachr0n0/Hands-on-Linux-course).
